From plan to results, in four stages

01 Plan

A strategy built from your business

Describe the business and its customer once. Munshi maps the terms your customers search onto content pillars you approve, then proposes what to write each week.

02 Write

Articles and posts that come out ready

Long-form articles drafted against your keywords and scored for both Google and AI answer engines. One idea becomes a post shaped for each network's limits.

03 Publish

Approved once, shipped everywhere

Articles are written into your CMS in its own format. Posts go out on schedule. Nothing unapproved ever ships.

04 Track

Every channel's numbers in one view

Reach, growth, and what worked, captured daily. Where a network reports nothing, Munshi says so instead of showing a zero.
